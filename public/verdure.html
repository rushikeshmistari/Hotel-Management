About Verdure

Verdure opened in 2018 in a converted greenhouse nursery, and the plants never really left — they just moved in with us. Every room looks onto the courtyard garden, and the restaurant runs on what's in season a short walk from the kitchen door.

We keep the hotel small on purpose. A handful of rooms means the front desk knows your name by the second morning, and the garden never feels crowded, even in the evenings when most guests drift out to it with a book.
